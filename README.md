# Engine Testers

A small browser prototype for Second Shift, a desert backroad gas station and repair shop.

## Run

```sh
npm install
npm run dev
```

Use WASD or the touch controls to move. Click the scene and move the mouse to look around; press Esc to release the mouse. E clocks in at Motor Works and operates the physical wall switch to open the bay doors. NPC traffic may pull into the gas station to refuel, and a tow truck delivers a wreck to Northside Salvage.

Use E at the garage computer to buy parts, accept client jobs, or purchase new, used, and salvage cars. Collect tools from the workbench, select them with 1–3, and press E near the Bay 01 car to repair it. Completed jobs pay into the shop account. Purchased cars are delivered to the customer lot.

After a repair, press E near a car to drive it. WASD accelerates, reverses, and steers; hold Space while steering to drift or hold W+Space for a burnout. Press E when stopped to park. On mobile, drag to look, use the touch controls to walk or drive, and tap Use or Drift.
