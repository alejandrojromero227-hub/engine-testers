# Engine Testers

A small browser prototype for Second Shift, a desert backroad gas station and repair shop.

## Run

```sh
npm install
npm run dev
```

Use WASD or the touch controls to move. Click the scene and move the mouse to look around; press Esc to release the mouse. Press E by the garage to clock in, then use E at the wall switch to open the bay doors. Use E at the workbench computer to buy parts, accept client jobs, or shop new, used, and salvage cars. Collect tools from the workbench, select them with 1–3, and press E near the Bay 01 car to repair it. Once it is repaired, press E near it to drive; nearby traffic cars can also be entered with E. While driving, WASD accelerates, reverses, and steers; hold Space while steering to drift or hold W+Space for a burnout. Press E when stopped to park. Mobile players can drag to look, use touch controls to move and drive, and tap Use or Drift.
